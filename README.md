# Additional Energy Projects

This repository contains a collection of academic projects developed throughout my studies in **Sustainable Energy Systems**.

The projects cover different areas of energy engineering, including:

- Power Systems stability analysis
- CHP thermo-econonomic optimisation
- Energy system modelling and optimization
- Energy policy analysis
- Energy efficiency
- Life Cycle Assessment (LCA)
- Energy storage
- Sustainable buildings
- Polygeneration systems
- Data-center sustainability

For most projects, the repository contains the **final PDF report**.  
For selected modelling and optimization projects, the corresponding **model files and inputs** are also included.

---

### Power System Stability and Control

Analysis of **power system dynamics and stability** using MATLAB Simulink.

The project investigates synchronous generator behaviour under different operating conditions, with particular attention to frequency stability, system inertia, active and reactive power regulation, droop control, and the interaction between synchronous generators and Voltage Source Converters (VSCs).

**Main topics:**  
`Power System Dynamics · Synchronous Generators · Frequency Stability · System Inertia · Droop Control · Voltage Source Converters · MATLAB Simulink`

**Files included:** PDF report.

---

### Thermal Conversion and Industrial Energy Systems Analysis

Thermo-economic optimization of a **gas turbine power plant** using MATLAB.

The project optimizes key plant design parameters while considering investment, maintenance and fuel costs. The analysis is extended through sensitivity studies of methane prices and CO/NOx emission penalties to investigate how economic and environmental signals influence optimal plant design.

**Main topics:**  
`MATLAB · fmincon · Thermo-Economic Optimization · Gas Turbines · Industrial Energy Systems · Sensitivity Analysis · Emission Costs`

**Files included:**  
- PDF report
- MATLAB scripts for optimization and sensitivity analyses

`Life Cycle Assessment · BESS · Hydrogen Storage · OpenLCA · Ecoinvent · LCOS · Renewable Energy`

### Energy Efficiency Audit for a Supermarket in Alaska

Energy audit and thermoeconomic assessment of a reference supermarket located in **Fairbanks, Alaska**.

The project investigates the building's energy consumption and evaluates potential efficiency improvements involving HVAC, refrigeration, lighting, the building envelope and battery storage.

**Main topics:**  
`Energy Efficiency · Energy Audit · Thermoeconomics · Exergy · HVAC · Building Energy`

**Files included:** PDF report.

### Policies Impact on the Netherlands Energy System using TIMES

Long-term energy-system modelling project evaluating how different **energy and climate policies** could influence the development of the Dutch electricity system between 2025 and 2050.

The analysis considers policy instruments such as:

- Carbon taxation
- CO₂ emission caps
- Renewable-energy targets
- Technology investment mandates
- Renewable-energy subsidies
- Carbon-tax revenue recycling
- 24/7 carbon-free electricity requirements

The system is modelled using the **TIMES framework through VEDA**, considering generation technologies, storage, electricity imports, capacity expansion, emissions and system costs.

**Main topics:**  
`TIMES · VEDA · Energy Policy · Energy System Optimization · Decarbonization · Scenario Analysis`

**Files included:**  
- PDF report
- TIMES / VEDA model files and related inputs

### Feasibility Study of a Thermal Energy Storage System in Sweden

Techno-economic feasibility study of an **ice-based Thermal Energy Storage (TES)** system for an industrial cooling application in Sweden.

Different operating strategies are evaluated to determine how thermal storage can shift chiller operation toward lower-price periods while reducing peak demand and maintaining the required cooling supply.

**Main topics:**  
`Thermal Energy Storage · Ice Storage · Industrial Cooling · Load Shifting · Techno-Economic Analysis`

**Files included:** PDF report.

### The Energy Cost of Not Acting Now – A Case Study on Google Data Centers

Sustainability and energy analysis of **Google data centers**, focusing on the potential consequences of delaying action on energy and climate objectives.

The study considers trends in electricity demand, carbon emissions, carbon-free energy procurement, cooling requirements and the increasing energy demand associated with data-center and AI growth.

**Main topics:**  
`Data Centers · Energy Consumption · Sustainability · Carbon-Free Energy · Climate Strategy · Energy Economics`

**Files included:** PDF report.

### Residential Electricity Cost Optimization using GAMS

Optimization of residential electricity consumption using **GAMS** and Day-Ahead electricity prices.

The model schedules flexible household loads, including appliances, HVAC and EV charging, to minimize the daily electricity bill while respecting operating-time and availability constraints.

The optimization is formulated as a **Mixed-Integer Programming (MIP)** problem.

**Main topics:**  
`GAMS · Mixed-Integer Programming · Demand-Side Management · Electricity Markets · EV Charging · Energy Optimization`

**Files included:**  
- PDF report
- GAMS model files

### Polygeneration System Design and Optimization

Design and optimization of a small-scale **polygeneration energy system** for a community sports and swimming facility in Brisbane, Australia.

The project progressively evaluates different system configurations combining:

- Solar PV
- Biomass CHP
- Battery Energy Storage
- Grid connection
- Heating, cooling and electricity demand

The configurations are compared using technical and economic indicators such as energy balance, investment costs, LCOE and payback period.

**Main topics:**  
`Polygeneration · Solar PV · Biomass CHP · Battery Storage · Energy System Design · Techno-Economic Analysis`

**Files included:**  
- PDF report
- Energy-system model files and supporting inputs

---

### Sustainable Building Design and LEED Assessment

Design and sustainability assessment of an office building located at the **KTH campus in Stockholm**.

The project evaluates the building using the **LEED v4** framework and investigates energy performance, HVAC operation, indoor environmental quality, water efficiency, renewable generation and other green-building strategies.

Building energy performance is assessed using **IDA ICE** simulations.

**Main topics:**  
`LEED · Sustainable Buildings · IDA ICE · Building Energy · HVAC · Indoor Environmental Quality · Solar PV`

**Files included:** PDF report.
